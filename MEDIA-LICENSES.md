## Production recommendation

Before the final website launch, download the selected images, optimise them as WebP or AVIF, store them inside `public/images`, and replace the remote URLs in `components/media/media-library.ts`.

This avoids relying on a third-party image host and gives Vikvar control over caching, cropping, performance, and availability.
